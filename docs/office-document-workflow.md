# Office document queues and final action

Updated 2026-10-19.

- **My Document**: office-origin registrations held by their encoding office with no dispatch history. The queue and action areas show Send and Delete; titles open details. Outside intake remains Received. After a dispatch, a document never regains Delete even when returned to its source.
- **Awaiting Receipt**: sent documents at their pending destination. Actions are View, Receive and Return. Return requires a reason and sends back to the exact last sender without recording a receipt at the rejecting office.
- **Outgoing**: only the latest handoffs sent by the selected office and still awaiting destination receipt. The list, pagination total, navigation count, and own-office dashboard count use the same rule. Receipt removes the handoff from Outgoing immediately; earlier senders retain access through All Timeline. A subsequent forward or return belongs only to its latest sender until receipt, including a return made before the original handoff was received. All-office monitoring shows only these pending handoffs. Records' Acted archive remains available after owner receipt.
- **Received**: ordinary confirmed handoffs and outside intake held by the office. Own documents returned after a real dispatch also belong here. PAD decision results received by Records are excluded from this list and its count and appear in For Action instead.
- **Returned**: issue-related handoffs made through the Return button, with their sidebar badge and current destination tracking retained. Automatic PAD decision dispatches are excluded using their review/result-route relationship, including existing records. A PAD Return before receipt remains an issue return that Records can receive, correct, and send again.
- **PAD decisions at Records**: arrive in **Awaiting Receipt**, with View and Receive. The existing receipt modal offers standard and custom remarks. Receipt records the receiving user and time, removes PAD's Outgoing handoff, and opens **For Action**, where Act becomes available. The separate Returned from PAD menu/card is removed; old frontend links open Awaiting Receipt. The legacy `pad_results` API remains available for compatibility.
- **For Action** (`for_action`): the active Central Records office's received PAD decisions awaiting Records processing, including Approved, Disapproved, and Returned for correction. It has its own sidebar and dashboard counts, stays local under monitoring filters, and excludes pending receipts and owner releases. Act removes the item and its count, while Acted retains the milestone. Previously received unclassified PAD handoffs stay accessible here with Decision not recorded. Records' overview aggregates ordinary received work and For Action items without changing their separate queue counts. No migration or live-record rewrite is needed.
- **Act**: available only to the active OVP Central Records module after its separate receipt of the PAD decision. Select an active owner office and record the action taken; no repeated receipt checkbox is required. An existing attachment and notes on the latest PAD dispatch remain required. The Act event and owner dispatch commit together; failure retains the earlier confirmed receipt. The API rejects Act on pending handoffs, including old combined receipt/action payloads.
- **Acted**: the archive of Records actions and dispatches to owners. New releases remain physically in transit until the selected owner confirms receipt. Records retains its Acted archive after delivery. The Act dispatch ends Records' office stay and starts a separately measured transit interval. Earlier terminal Acted records remain closed without being rewritten.
- **Delivered / completed**: **Delivered** records the actual owner hard-copy receipt of a released PAD result; it is not set merely by dispatching. **Completed** closes processing without a delivery handoff. Owner receipt also closes the release route and records its delivery time. A result requiring correction remains available to its owner for correction and resubmission on the same file code, while its earlier Records action and delivered instructions remain in history.
- **All Timeline**: OVP PAD and OVP Central Records default to the full registry across all offices, with matching global sidebar/dashboard totals and optional office filtering. Ordinary office lists/counts include only documents the office encoded or actually dispatched, using registration and dispatch history; older records without registration events fall back to their source office. Merely receiving a document does not add it to an ordinary office's All Timeline. Receive-only final recipients retain Awaiting Receipt, Delivered / completed, and direct detail/history access from those queues. Global PAD timeline reading does not grant outside-intake authority or another office's actions. Switching back to an ordinary module on a multi-role identity restores ordinary visibility. Working queues retain their existing office scope.

All guards apply in the shared API for both /api/documents and /api/doc_router/documents. Deletion locks and rechecks the document, removes unsent registration details and the attached file, and leaves annual numbering unchanged. No database migration or live-document rewrite is needed. New owner releases use dispatched `acted` history events, keeping Records' action separate from physical routing status.

Validation: 201 isolated office-routing checks (including Outgoing and ordinary All Timeline transitions, HTTP receipt, and 12 concurrent registrations), 253 isolated PAD-review checks (including global OVP timelines, office visibility, separate queue counts, For Action, and final delivery), 69 presidential monitoring checks, 99 frontend tests, the production build, and sample browser verification passed. No real document is transferred, acted on or deleted for verification.
